## 1. NRD model contract (`src/nedm/traverse/nrd_model.py`, `map_crop.py`)

- `WP2MapModel.forward(z1, token, act)` concatenates `[z1, token, act]` per frame and runs a causal `ContinuousTransformer` (block_size 16 = 16-frame context); heads return `(delta z1 normalised, power normalised, next-token or None)` (`nrd_model.py:53-71`). Trained config: 6 layers, 8 heads, 256 embd, 5.2 M params (`artifacts/traverse/wp2_mapv2_index_amd/config.json`).
- z1 is the `tire_normal_force_omega` preset (15-D) or `_pt` (17-D: + engine speed, motorshaft torque), field order in `src/nedm/training/constants.py:3-55`; kept z-scored inside the model (`nrd_model.py:7-9`). Action is 3-D `[steer, throttle, brake]` z-scored with dataset stats.
- Token = `MapCropper`: an 8x8 ego-aligned window (+/-5 m) of the frozen WP1 encoder's stage-2 map (64 x 64 x 64 per episode, fp16 cache key `map_v2`), sampled through the camera projection (the arena heightmap serves only the geometry), then 1x1 conv 64->16 + Linear(1024->256) (`map_crop.py:39-58, 87-107`). The model has no raw terrain input and no domain input.
- Pose is integrated outside the network: yaw += dt*yaw_rate, then world velocity uses the new yaw with body vx, vy; planar only, `DT_S = 0.05` (`nrd_model.py:29-42`).
- Checkpoint payload keys: `model`, `config` (block_size, n_layer, n_head, n_embd, dropout, bias, head_hidden_dim), `map_mode`, `normalization` (z1/z2/act/power mean+std), `step`, `metrics`, `z1_dim`, `z1_extra_cache`, `arena_ids`, `delta_scale` (`scripts/traverse_wp2_train_map.py:691-695`). `load_map_model(ckpt, arena_dir, device)` rebuilds the cropper for the requested arena and drops the checkpoint's heightmap buffer (`nrd_model.py:74-95`); the tracker env refuses non-`index` checkpoints (`tracker_env.py:223-224`).

## 2. Dataset / cache contract (`nrd_data.py`) and a new f104/CRM cache

- Per-episode `.npz`: `z1 (T,15|17)`, `act (T,3)`, `pose (T,3)`, `power (T,1)` kW, optional `z2`, `terrain (T,64)`, `map`/`map_v2 (64,64,64)` fp16; schema v2 adds `arena`, `status`, `route_{waypoints,speeds,headings,stations}` (`nrd_data.py:104-114`; `scripts/traverse_wp7_build_cache.py:119-120`). `cache_manifest.json`: `episodes`, and for v2 `arena_of`, `arenas`, `status_of`, `schema: 2`. Variable-length episodes are padded and masked via `valid` (`nrd_data.py:80-135`). Normaliser = per-channel z-score fitted on the train split (`nrd_data.py:194-212`). Splits: fixed-seed episode permutation 0.7/0.15 (`nrd_data.py:24-35`) or by arena (`train_map.py:524-553`); no route-group split exists.
- Row timing (cache and Chrono runner agree): state after `Synchronize` at the first substep, the action applied in that interval (`scripts/traverse_wp3_chrono_eval.py:296-298, 473-481`). The f104 rigid runner and the CRM collector match (`scripts/traverse_fdm_rgbd_diverse_chrono.py:220-235, 393`; `scripts/crm_collect.py:232-245`), BUT their `ChPathFollowerDriver` is re-synchronised every physics substep (2 ms rigid, 0.5 ms CRM) with a per-substep 2.0/s steering clamp, so the logged action is the first-substep PID output, not a held action; the plan's step-2 statement is confirmed. Only the tracker-driven caches (`scripts/traverse_wp4_collect_tracker_episodes.py:91-96`) are true zero-order-hold transitions.
- f104 rigid and CRM recordings (verified on `fdm_f104_50h_20260909/production_v4/runs/*` and `crm_f104_v1/demo_v1/run_*`): `trajectory.npz` = `state (T,17)` in the `_pt` preset order, `action (T,3)`, `pose (T,3)`, `power_kw (T,)`, `contact_n`, `parked`, `state_fields`, `dt_s 0.05`; `command_reference.npz` = `reference_{waypoints,stations,speeds,headings}` at ~0.5 m spacing (measured 0.497-0.68 m) plus per-interval `desired_speed_mps`; `anchor_state.npz` `history (16,24)` (`src/nedm/traverse/fdm_data.py:79-93`); `case.json` carries a `layout` dict in the `EpisodeLayout` schema with `assets: []`; arena `arena_f104_50h_v1` is byte-identical to `arena_f104`. CRM adds `crm_extra.npz` (sinkage, slip, FSI fx). Neither has a camera frame or an encoded `map_v2`.
- Raw elevation crop instead of the encoder token: architecturally trivial (the token is just a tensor input) and empirically favourable: the privileged 8x8 +/-6 m relative-height patch (`scripts/traverse_wp2_add_terrain.py:24-26`) was the CEILING in WP2 Batch C (z1mae@1 s 0.221 vs learned crop 0.248 vs state-only 0.328; wp2 notes lines 242-249). Sampling points already exist (`map_crop.py:80-85`, `_terrain_height` 60-78). The 09-08 audit's 32x32 crop module `terrain_crop.py` is NOT in this worktree (only in `/home/harry/NeDM/src/nedm/traverse/`). A raw crop is domain-blind (same BMP in both domains) and static (no CRM ruts), so domain identity must come from a tag or history.

## 3. Tracker (`tracker_env.py`, `scripts/traverse_wp3_train_tracker.py`)

- 38-D observation (`tracker_env.py:267, 478-490`): [0] e_along/10, [1] e_ct/10, [2] e_h/pi to the nearest waypoint in a -2..+40 waypoint window (`365-385`); [3:33] 10 preview points at 1 m spacing: (bx/10, by/10, v_ref/5) in body frame (`387-398`); [33] vx/10, [34] yaw rate (rad/s, unscaled); [35:38] last applied physical action. `obs_history_steps>0` appends normalised z1 frames (hist4 variant), never past actions.
- Action squash: `clamp(center + scale*tanh(a), low, high)` with center = dataset action mean ([-0.003, 0.200, 0.020]), scale [1.0, 0.7, 0.5], low [-1,0,0], high [1,1,1] (`341-343`; `wp3_tracker_v1/policy_meta.json`) so throttle spans ~[0, 0.9], brake ~[0, 0.52]. Steering-rate clamp 0.1 per step (= collectors' 2.0/s) applied during training (`404-407`) and in Chrono (`chrono_eval.py:139-140`).
- Reward (`413-421`): exp(-(2 (e_ct/1)^2 + 0.8 (e_h/0.35)^2 + 0.5 (e_v/1)^2)) - 0.2 |da|^2 - 0.05 throttle*brake. Terminations: |e_ct|>6 m, |roll|>0.6, |pitch|>0.4 rad, non-finite; time-out at fragment end or route end bootstraps (`423-430`).
- Fragments (`296-338`): random bank episode, length U[20,60] steps (1-3 s), start uniform in [16, active_end - len] where active_end is the last frame >3 m of arc from the route end (`188-193`); the 16-frame z1/action/pose context and crop tokens come from the recording; `last_actions` = recorded action at start-1. Per-env maps cost 2.1 GB GPU at 2048 envs (`171, 248`).
- PPO (`train_tracker.py:59-71, 121-148`): rsl-rl-lib 2.2.4 `OnPolicyRunner`, 2048 envs x 64 steps, 5 epochs x 8 minibatches, lr 3e-4 adaptive KL 0.01, entropy 3e-3, clip 0.2, gamma 0.99, lambda 0.95, grad-norm 1.0, actor/critic [512,256,128] ELU, init noise 0.7, empirical obs normalisation, 1000 iterations. Recorded time: 14 min per 1000 iterations on one MI350 (wp3 notes line 54), 19 min per retrain (wp4 notes line 1909). No RTX 5090 timing exists; the local `nedm` env (torch 2.12+cu130, rsl-rl-lib 2.2.4, 5090 32 GB) can run it.
- `policy_meta.json` is required by the Chrono evaluator (`chrono_eval.py:116`) but not written by the training script (writes only `env_cfg.json`, `train_cfg.json`, `train_tracker.py:196-197`); no writer exists anywhere in `scripts/` or `src/` of this worktree (grep: only `traverse_wp3_chrono_eval.py` and `traverse_wp9_bowl_tasks.py` read it).

## 4. Chrono evaluator (`scripts/traverse_wp3_chrono_eval.py`)

- One process per (controller, route) via `spawn` Pool, default 12 procs, `maxtasksperchild=1` (`730`); rows appended to `rows.jsonl`, `summary.json` aggregated.
- 20 Hz loop: `CTRL_DT_S 0.05`, substeps = 25 at the 2 ms rigid step (`287-288`; `scene.py:58`); 0.8 s braked settle; per tick the observation is rebuilt from Chrono's TRUE pose/speed (or camera/fused localiser) and the policy's command is written into manual `DriverInputs` and HELD for all substeps (zero-order hold, `469-470`). The `follower` arm is the same `ChPathFollowerDriver` as the f104/CRM collectors (gains 0.8 / 0.6, 0.05, look-ahead 5 m, `326-329` vs `crm_collect.py:7`), synchronised every substep (`459-467`).
- Routes: recorded `routes/<key>.npz`, oracle sweep candidates, `--route-file`, or `--tasks-file` entries `{key, meta_path, candidate, route{waypoints,speeds,headings,stations}, horizon_s, off_route_m, stall_abort_s, park_s, record, controller}` (`589-604`); `v_ref` = route speed at the nearest waypoint (`92-97`).
- Metrics (`555-571`): completion, time_s, energy_kj, mean/p95/max |ct|, speed/heading error, contact, roll/pitch, min tire Fz, stall_s, unloaded/airborne, clearance, steer_rate_max. No RMSE.
- Layout is rebuilt from `meta["layout"]` via `EpisodeLayout.from_json` (`256-257`); the default task builder is the arena_v1 store family (`621-625`), but `--tasks-file` accepts any `meta_path`. f104 rigid works as-is: `meta_path = case.json`, `--arena assets/traverse/arena_f104_50h_v1`, routes from `command_reference.npz` with keys renamed, horizon above 25 s. CRM is NOT supported: `run_one` builds `RigidTerrain` + TMEASY and steps `hmmwv.Advance` (`scene.py:76-96, 375`; `chrono_eval.py:491-492`); CRM needs `veh.CRMTerrain`, RIGID_MESH tyres, chassis collision off, 0.5 ms step (100 substeps) and `terrain.Advance` only (`crm_collect.py:176-190, 269-270`). `crm_collect.run` hard-codes the path follower (`190`) and exposes only `scene_hook`/`frame_hook` (`193, 258-260`).

## 5. Actual Chrono result (`artifacts/traverse/wp3_chrono_eval_v1/rows.jsonl`)

93 valid rows (one non-JSON line), 31 layouts fully paired across `wp3_tracker_v1`, `wp3_tracker_v1_s2`, `follower`, candidate `recorded` only, all completed, arena_v1, mean route 44 m, ~11 s. Paired means: mean |ct| 0.029 (v1) / 0.042 (s2) vs 0.075 m follower (v1-follower -0.046 +/- 0.008 SE, lower on 28/31; s2 -0.033 +/- 0.008, 21/31); p95 0.071 / 0.116 vs 0.255; max 0.126 / 0.440 vs 0.314; time 11.22 / 11.34 vs 11.09 s; energy 184 / 208 vs 158 kJ (+16 % / +32 %, worse on 29/31); speed error 0.36 / 0.34 vs 0.42 m/s. The tracker beat the same PID on cross-track and tied on completion, at an energy cost, on the easy arena (slope cap 0.36 vs 0.60 on f104, no stalls): evidence that the recipe transfers, not evidence for f104/CRM.

## Gaps / unknowns

- No RTX 5090 throughput measurement; MI350 numbers only.
- `policy_meta.json` writer missing; must be reproduced for new runs.
- No route-group-aware split; no domain tag anywhere; no past-action history in the observation.
- The trainer's stall validation needs `events.json` from `traverse_wp7_stall_diagnosis.py` (`train_map.py:362-413, 605-618`); f104 outcomes carry `bounded_blockage_v1_windows` instead. The 09-08 audit replay script and `terrain_crop.py` exist only in `/home/harry/NeDM`.
- `rollout_eval` reports z1 MAE / pose error, not signed displacement or stalled-vs-moving splits (`train_map.py:280-358`).
- Chrono evaluator with `obs_history_steps>0` builds z1 history from the 15-D preset (`271, 427`), mismatching a 17-D policy.
- Rigid f104 hours in production_v2-v4 were not counted here.

## What must change for the plan

1. Converter: recordings -> schema-v2 cache (`z1=state`, `act=action`, `pose`, `power=power_kw[:,None]`, `status` from `outcome.json`, `arena`, `route_*` from `command_reference.npz`, plus a `domain` scalar and group id) with a v2 manifest.
2. Replace `MapCropper` by a raw relative-elevation cropper (8x8 or 16x16, +/-5-6 m, heights minus vehicle ground height, /2 m) and add a domain tag input; plumb both through `WP2MapModel`, `MapBatcher.sample`, `rollout_loss`, `TraverseTrackingEnv.crop/_nn_step` (dropping the per-env map bank).
3. Group-aware split (case id prefix) in `nrd_data`/trainer.
4. Free-rollout validation on held-out recorded actions: signed displacement, vx/yaw response, stalled vs moving windows, measured vs fed-back state, at the RL horizon (60 steps).
5. Tracker: past-action block in the obs (env + `PolicyController`), action centre from f104 data, PID-imitation warm start (supervised init of `ActorCritic` via `physical_to_policy`), and a `policy_meta.json` writer.
6. Chrono: f104 tasks-file builder from `case.json`/`command_reference.npz`; a CRM runner with policy injection (extend `crm_collect.run` with a controller callback holding manual inputs over the 100 substeps); RMSE and paired reporting.
